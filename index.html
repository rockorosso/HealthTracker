About This Data
  • Source: Apple Health export (data from Feb 2018). Devices: iPhone, Zepp Life band via the Google Health app, an old Apple Watch (2018). Daily values use the highest single source per day, so devices are not double-counted.
  • Steps come mostly from the wrist band, which can count arm movement (e.g. gym sessions) as steps; distance comes from the iPhone. That explains high step counts next to a lower distance.
  • Heart-rate and sleep-stage data only start in Sep 2026 (no 2025 comparison yet). Days without the band (e.g. 8–11 Nov 2026) have no HR or sleep.
  • Nights under 3 h (e.g. 14 Nov 2026, 0.5 h) are treated as "band not worn" and excluded from averages.
  • No blood pressure, VO₂ max or recent HRV in the export. Weight is entered manually on the Weight page (last: 59 kg at the GP, 18 Nov 2026).
  • Gym sessions (sets, loads) are kept in the training log, not here.

Cardiovascular Risk — Needs Attention
High Priority

After improving to 2.87 mmol/L in Jun 2026, LDL rebounded to 4.88 mmol/L (24 Nov 2026) — the highest on record. Total cholesterol 6.86, non-HDL 5.31 (target <3.80), cholesterol/HDL ratio 4.43 (limit 4.20). TG 1.75 is just above range. HDL 1.55 is good and Lp(a) is low (12.4 nmol/L). The lab noted that sampling conditions were not fully met, so a repeat fasting panel is worth asking for.

Context: On 26 Nov 2026 the GP suggested going back to Lipanthyl (fenofibrate), which Pablo took until ~2024; in Oct 2024 his values were much better (TC 4.74, LDL 2.33). Fenofibrate mainly lowers TG (and uric acid) and lowers LDL only modestly. With Gilbert's + SLCO1B1 *5, statins need extra caution, so LDL-specific options without SLCO1B1 interaction (e.g. ezetimibe) are worth raising. The 26 Nov ultrasound showed a normal gallbladder, which is relevant because fibrates can promote gallstones.

💊
Vitamin B12 — Persistently Elevated
Monitor

B12 has been 2–3× above the upper limit across all tests: 882, 882, 842, 891 pmol/L (range: 145–569). This is consistent and significant. Possible causes include B12 supplementation, liver-related storage issues, or rarely a myeloproliferative condition. Your liver enzymes are mostly normal (AST briefly elevated Oct 2024).

Intersection with genetics: Your MTRR A66G +/- variant reduces B12 recycling efficiency — the body may compensate by accumulating more. However at these levels, it's worth discussing with your doctor whether you are supplementing B12 and if so, whether to reduce the dose.

🫁
Bilirubin — Recurrent Mild Elevation
Monitor

Bilirubin was elevated in Feb 2023, Mar 2024, Oct 2025 and May 2026 (26.9–27.2), and reached 32.1 in Nov 2026, with conjugated bilirubin 8.3 (limit 5.0). ALT, AST, GGT and ALP are all normal, FIB-4 is 0.72 (low fibrosis risk), and the 26 Nov ultrasound showed a normal liver with no bile-duct dilation. This fits Gilbert's syndrome (confirmed, UGT1A1 variant), which rises with fasting, illness and stress — the Nov sample followed a diarrhoea episode.

Recommendation: No treatment needed. Mention the slightly high conjugated fraction to your doctor, since Gilbert's mainly raises the unconjugated part.
